My Hobbies

When I’m not coding, I enjoy a variety of hobbies that keep me active and engaged. Here are a few of my favorites:

  • Playing Guitar: I’ve been playing guitar for over 10 years and love learning new songs and techniques. I find it relaxing and rewarding to create music and express myself through my playing.
  • Reading: I’m a big fan of science fiction and fantasy novels, and I love getting lost in a good book. Reading helps me unwind and escape from the stresses of everyday life.
  • Photography: I’m an amateur photographer and enjoy capturing moments and memories through my lens. Photography allows me to express my creativity and share my perspective with others.
  • Video Games: I’m a huge fan of video games! Whether it’s exploring open worlds, tackling intense challenges, or just immersing myself in a great story, gaming is one of my favorite ways to unwind and have fun. I love the creativity, strategy, and excitement that video games bring, and there’s always something new to experience. Whether solo or multiplayer, gaming is a big part of what I enjoy!
  • TV and Movies: I absolutely love watching movies and series! Whether it's a gripping thriller, a mind-bending sci-fi, or a feel-good comedy, I enjoy getting lost in different stories and exploring new worlds. There's something about great storytelling, compelling characters, and unexpected twists that keeps me hooked. It’s my favorite way to relax and unwind.
